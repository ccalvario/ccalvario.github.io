Privacy Policy

Sokkermetrics · Effective date: October 14, 2026 · Author: Cuauhtemoc Calvario

This Privacy Policy explains how the Sokkermetrics mobile application (the “App”), developed by Cuauhtemoc Calvario (“I”, “me”), handles information when you use it. Sokkermetrics is an unofficial companion app for the online football manager game Sokker that lets you track your squad, juniors, training and team over time. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Sokker.

In short:

  • I do not run any server. Your Sokker login and your synced team data stay on your device.
  • The App talks to sokker.org to download your team data, using your own Sokker account.
  • Optional backups go to a file you choose or to a hidden App folder in your own Google Drive.
  • On Android, the App uses Google services: Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics and Google AdMob (ads), which collect some device data as described below.
  • I do not sell your personal information.

1. Information the App handles

1.1 Your Sokker account login

To sync your data, you log in with your sokker.org username and password. These are sent directly from your device to sokker.org over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection. They are never sent to me or to any other party.

Your username and team ID are kept on your device so the App can show your data. If you tick Remember me, your password is also kept, so you do not need to type it on every sync. All of these are stored in your device’s secure storage (the Android Keystore or iOS Keychain). Logging out removes them.

1.2 Sokker game data

The App downloads data about your club from the Sokker XML interface at sokker.org: players and juniors (skills, values, salaries, injuries, cards, nationality), trainers, team details, kits, weekly figures and training reports. This data is saved in a database on your device so you can see how it changes week by week, including players and juniors who have left your club. It is not uploaded anywhere unless you make a backup (see below).

The data you get from sokker.org is governed by Sokker’s own terms and privacy policy.

1.3 App settings

Preferences such as your chosen theme and whether Google Drive backup is connected are stored on your device only.

2. Backups

2.1 Backup to a file

You can save a copy of the App’s database to a file and restore it later, for example to move to a new device. The file is written to, or read from, a location you pick with your device’s file picker. The App does not access any other files. You are responsible for where you keep that file and who you share it with.

2.2 Google Drive backup (optional, Android)

If you choose Connect Google Drive in Settings, you sign in with your Google account through Google Sign-In and grant the App the drive.appdata permission. This lets the App use only its own hidden application data folder in your Google Drive. The App cannot see, read or change any of your other Drive files.

  • The App stores a single backup copy of its database in that folder.
  • Your Google account email is shown in Settings so you know which account is connected. It is not stored anywhere else or sent to me.
  • The backup is held by Google under the Google Privacy Policy.
  • You can disconnect at any time in Settings. To delete the backup, open Google Drive → Settings → Manage apps, find Sokkermetrics and choose Delete hidden app data, or remove the App’s access in your Google Account permissions.

4. Advertising and your consent

The App shows a banner ad from Google AdMob. When you are in a region where the law requires it, such as the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the App asks for your consent through Google’s consent form before any ads are requested. No ads are requested until consent has been gathered.

You can change your choice at any time with Settings → Ads → Privacy options (shown when it applies in your region). You can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your device’s settings (on Android: Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Security & privacy → Privacy → Ads).

5. Device permissions

  • Internet access: to reach sokker.org, Google Drive, Firebase and AdMob.
  • Advertising ID (Android, AD_ID): read by Google AdMob and Firebase Analytics to show and measure ads and to measure App usage, as described above.
  • Android Privacy Sandbox ad services (Topics and Attribution APIs, ACCESS_ADSERVICES_*): added by the Google Mobile Ads and Firebase Analytics SDKs, and used for ad relevance and measurement where your device supports them. You can turn these off in your device’s ad privacy settings.
  • Install referrer (Google Play): used by Firebase Analytics to know where the App was installed from.

The App does not ask for access to your contacts, camera, microphone, photos or precise location.

6. How long data is kept

  • On your device: your login and game data stay until you log out (login), clear the App’s data, or uninstall the App.
  • Google Drive backup: stays in your Drive until you delete it as described in section 2.2.
  • Firebase Analytics: kept according to the retention set for the project, at most 14 months for user-level data.
  • Firebase Crashlytics: crash reports are kept for 90 days.
  • AdMob: kept according to Google’s policies.

7. Sharing of information

I do not sell, rent or trade your personal information. Information is only shared with the service providers listed in section 3, for the purposes described there, or where required by law.

8. Security

Your Sokker login is kept in your device’s secure, encrypted storage, and all network traffic uses HTTPS. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so I cannot guarantee absolute security, but I take reasonable steps to protect your information.

9. Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU or UK, or the CCPA/CPRA in California), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal data, to object to its processing, to data portability, and to withdraw consent. Most of the data the App handles is on your device and under your control:

  • Log out to remove your stored Sokker login.
  • Clear the App’s data or uninstall it to remove all locally stored data.
  • Disconnect Google Drive and delete the hidden backup as described in section 2.2.
  • Change your ad consent with Settings → Ads → Privacy options, and manage your advertising ID in your device settings.

For data held by Google (Firebase and AdMob), I can help with requests connected to the App instance identifiers. Contact me at the address below. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

10. Children’s privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). I do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, please contact me and I will help remove it.

11. Changes to this policy

I may update this Privacy Policy when the App’s features or services change. The new version will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. Please review it from time to time.
