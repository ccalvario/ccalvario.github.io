Habitat Grid Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 29, 2026

Introduction

Habitat Grid does not ask for your name, email or location, and we never sell your data. This policy explains what limited information the game and its partner services handle, and why.

Habitat Grid (Android package com.ccalvario.habitatgrid) is a single-player puzzle game published by Cuauhtemoc Calvario ("we", "us"). This policy applies to the game on Google Play and to any updates. By playing, you agree to the practices described here.

How we use information and your ad choices

We use the data above only to run the game, show ads that fund it, and fix bugs. We do not sell personal information or use it for any other purpose.

  • Ads. Habitat Grid shows banner, interstitial and optional rewarded video ads through Google AdMob. Ads may be personalized based on your advertising ID unless you opt out.
  • Consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland). On first launch, the game shows Google's consent form before any ad is requested. To change your choice later, clear the game's data (Android Settings > Apps > Habitat Grid > Storage > Clear data) and the consent form will appear again on next launch, or email us and we will help.
  • Opting out of personalized ads. On Android, open Settings > Google > Ads (or Settings > Privacy > Ads) and delete or reset your advertising ID. You will still see ads, but they will be less relevant.
  • Leaderboards and cloud save. These work only if you sign in to Google Play Games. You can sign out or manage your profile in the Google Play Games app.

Children, retention and security

Children. Habitat Grid is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, contact us and we will help remove it.

Retention and deletion.

  • Data saved on your device stays until you clear the app's data or uninstall the game.
  • Cloud saves and leaderboard entries are kept by Google Play Games until you delete them. You can delete your Play Games data for this game in the Google Play Games app or at myaccount.google.com.
  • Crash reports in Firebase Crashlytics are kept for 90 days. Analytics and advertising data are kept by Google under its own retention policies.

Security. Data sent to Google services travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS). No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we rely on Google's safeguards and collect as little as possible.

Your rights, changes and contact

Your rights. Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EEA and UK, or the CCPA in California), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict use of your personal data, to object to processing, and to withdraw consent. Because we do not hold an account or profile for you, most requests are best handled through the Google tools linked above. For anything else, email us and we will respond within 30 days. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

Legal bases (EEA/UK). We process data on the basis of your consent (personalized ads), our legitimate interest in keeping the game stable and funded (crash reports, analytics, non-personalized ads), and performance of the service you request (cloud save, leaderboards).

Changes. We may update this policy when the game changes. The date at the top shows the latest version, and significant changes will be noted in the game's store listing.
