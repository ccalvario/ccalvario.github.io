How to play

Easy to learn, with plenty to think about on every move.

  1. 1Tap a tile on the belt, then tap the grid to place it.
  2. 2Each new tile must go next to the last one you placed, diagonals included.
  3. 3Belt tiles cost coins. Slots further left cost more: 4, 3, 2 and 1 from left to right.
  4. 4Running low? Grass and desert are always available and give coins back, but they push the whole belt along.
  5. 5The game ends when the grid is full or you run out of moves.

Scoring guide

Tiles score from their neighbors above, below, left and right. Diagonals do not count.

  • Lake

    Always scores 1.

  • Tree

    Scores 1 per adjacent tree or lake.

  • Bear

    Scores 1 per adjacent fish, grass, tree or lake.

  • House

    Chains with neighboring houses. Each scores by build order in its chain: 1, 2, 4, 8, then 16.

  • Fish

    Scores 2 per adjacent lake.

  • Eagle

    Scores 1 per adjacent fish. Can be placed anywhere on the grid.

  • Mountain

    Scores 1 and resets the belt.

  • Grass

    Refunds 1 coin, scores 0, shifts the belt.

  • Desert

    Refunds 2 coins, scores 0, shifts the belt.
