Find the appliance that runs up the bill

The details screen splits your month into a chart and ranks every item by kWh. In this example an electric water heater used for one hour a day is half of the total, more than both TVs, the computer and the blender put together.

Start from a list of common appliances

You don't need to know the wattage of your fridge. Pick from more than 40 appliances with typical watts already filled in, grouped by TV, lights, fans, air conditioning, kitchen, home and computer. Search if you'd rather not browse.

Enter how you actually use it

Each item takes a name, how many you have, its watts, the days per month you use it and the time it runs each day. If the label on your appliance says something different from the preset, type your own number.

Enter one price per kWh, or set up the tiers your provider charges so the cost follows the way your bill is calculated.
